DIS-507 — Inventory tracking

Daily Summary posts one journal entry per period. This prototype adds the companion inventory posting, so quantity on hand moves in the books. Each sales product is matched to an inventory item. The match is stored once.

The mock follows the live Products, Mapping, Summaries, and Products and services settings screens. Two places can edit the match. Open either version, change a match, then switch. The other version shows the same saved match.

UX notes — why the match belongs on Products

Version A
Products page

The match editor lives on Products: accounting-item column, Not matched filter, bulk actions, Find matching products. Mapping keeps posting accounts and a banner when products are not matched.
Version B
Mapping page

A Products tab on Mapping is the editor. The Products page shows a Matched or Not matched badge and a link back.

No match saved in this browser yet. The sample starts with Kinipop Beanie matched.

What to try
  • Shared match: on Version A, open the accounting item on Kinipop Tote and pick an item. Choose “See it on Version B”. The Products tab shows the same match, and the Products page badge updates.
  • Find matching products: review three results before anything is saved — matched (SKU or exact name), multiple candidates (pick one), and no match.
  • Create items sits in the top bar after the ledger switcher, and this browser remembers it. true shows Create on each unmatched row, bulk create, the QuickBooks Online and Xero dialog, and the Sage Intacct / NetSuite “Currently in development” flow with “I need this”. false hides all of that. Matching stays manual, or via Find matching products. Both versions use the same switch.
  • Not matched: the banner says those quantities won't be adjusted. “Show not matched” filters the list.
  • Settings: gear icon → Products and services. Inventory tracking on turns the per-integration COGS toggles off and disables them, with a short hint. Turn it off and the COGS toggles work again.
  • Sync examples: Summaries shows a period where the journal entry and the inventory adjustment both synced, and a period where the journal entry synced and the adjustment failed with Attention required.
