DIS-547 — Shopify: reconcile all clearing accounts at once

Today's Shopify reconciliation, plus a new "Clearing accounts to reconcile" checklist before Run. It lists the store's clearing accounts from the Shopify sync settings (Shopify Payments, POS, Manual, Other). All are selected by default, and you can clear any account you don't need. This version follows the Story SET-1612 (draft v4).

What to try in the mock
  • All accounts (default): keep all four checked and click Run reconciliation. The results show an overview, one button per account with its status, and the "Outside clearing accounts" panel.
  • Some accounts: clear one or two accounts. The results show buttons only for the accounts you kept, plus the overview.
  • One account: keep only one account checked. The results show that account only, the same as today. With only Shopify Payments, the result is the same as today's Shopify reconciliation.
  • No account: clear all of them. Run reconciliation is not available until you select at least one account.
  • Xero: switch "Books" to Xero in the top bar. The Shopify side stays automatic. For the books side, you upload one Account Transactions report that covers the selected accounts (click the upload area to simulate it). QuickBooks Online stays fully automatic.
  • No POS account in settings: turn on the demo in the top bar. POS shows as unchecked and not available, with a warning and a link to the settings. In the results, POS payments show as "Unassigned".
Business rules shown
  • The list of clearing accounts comes from the store's Shopify sync settings as they are when the run starts. The user does not map accounts.
  • Available on every plan that has reconciliation.
  • PayPal and other payments that a different integration syncs: "Settled outside Shopify".
  • Gift cards and store credit: "Excluded".
  • Order types with no clearing account: "Unassigned", with a link to the settings.
  • The "Outside clearing accounts" panel shows when two or more accounts are selected. With one account, the screen stays the same as today's results.
  • POS order paid partly by card and partly by cash: the card part goes to Shopify Payments, the cash part goes to POS.
  • POS cash refunds are matched by refund ID, with a warning when the books and the settings use different accounts.
  • The period goes by payment date, not by order date.

The UI is identical to today's Transaction Reconciliation; NEW markers (toggle in the top bar) show what was added. All store names, IDs, and amounts are anonymized.
