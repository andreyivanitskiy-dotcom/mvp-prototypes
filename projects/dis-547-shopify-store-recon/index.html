DIS-547 — Shopify: reconcile all clearing accounts at once

Today's Shopify reconciliation, plus a new "What to reconcile" choice before Run: one clearing account of your choice (Shopify Payments, POS, Manual, or Other), or all clearing accounts of the store in one run. This version follows the Story SET-1612 (draft v3).

Open mock → Previous version (v3) Previous version (v2)
What to try in the mock
  • One clearing account: keep "One clearing account", pick Shopify Payments (same as today), POS, Manual, or Other, and click Run reconciliation. The results show that account only.
  • All clearing accounts: select "All clearing accounts of this store" and click Run reconciliation. The results show one button per account with its status, an overview, and the "Outside clearing accounts" panel.
  • Xero: switch "Books" to Xero in the top bar. The Shopify side stays automatic. For the books side, you upload one Account Transactions report that covers all clearing accounts (click the upload area to simulate it). QuickBooks Online stays fully automatic.
  • No POS account in settings: turn on the demo in the top bar. POS is not offered as a single account, the form shows that POS has no clearing account, and the results show POS payments as "Unassigned" with a link to the settings.
Business rules shown
  • The list of clearing accounts comes from the store's Shopify sync settings as they are when the run starts. The user does not map accounts.
  • Available on every plan that has reconciliation.
  • PayPal and other payments that a different integration syncs: "Settled outside Shopify".
  • Gift cards and store credit: "Excluded".
  • Order types with no clearing account: "Unassigned", with a link to the settings.
  • POS order paid partly by card and partly by cash: the card part goes to Shopify Payments, the cash part goes to POS.
  • POS cash refunds are matched by refund ID, with a warning when the books and the settings use different accounts.
  • The period goes by payment date, not by order date.

The UI is identical to today's Transaction Reconciliation; NEW markers (toggle in the top bar) show what was added. All store names, IDs, and amounts are anonymized.
